01 / WHAT WE DO

Evidence first. Claims second.

Viridian does not replace your training stack, eval framework or engineering team. We sit around the experiment and ask a narrower question: does the evidence support the decision you are about to make?

01

Comparability

We examine whether baseline and candidate results are actually comparable across datasets, versions, configuration, execution environment and evaluation method.

02

Evaluator integrity

We test whether judge, rubric or scoring changes quietly altered what the experiment measures — and whether reported gains survive that boundary.

03

Evidence provenance

We trace what produced the result, which identities and assumptions it depends on, and which parts of the historical evidence remain defensible.

02 / EXPERIMENTAL AUDIT

A bounded answer to a high-value question.

Our initial audit is deliberately narrow: one principal claim, one evidence package and one written assurance finding. We identify what the evidence supports, what it does not, and what must be re-run or qualified before the result can carry more weight.

04 / PRINCIPLES

We do not sell certainty.

We show you exactly what the evidence can justify.

01

No witness, no promotion. A stronger claim requires stronger, identified evidence.

02

No authority, no effect. A result cannot silently gain decision weight from computation alone.

03

Unknown stays unknown. Missing evidence is reported as a boundary, not converted into confidence.
